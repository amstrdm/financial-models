### ARS Pharmaceuticals: The Core Story

- **Ticker:** SPRY
- **Flagship Product:** Neffy (nasal spray epinephrine)
- **FDA Milestone:** Approval granted on August 12, clearing the main regulatory hurdle.
- **Goal:** Disrupt the $1B annual EpiPen market with an easier-to-use nasal delivery system.

---

### The Epinephrine Market & Neffy’s Advantages

1. **Needle Hesitancy**
    - EpiPen misuse and fear of injections cause many people to avoid filling or using prescriptions.
    - Neffy’s nasal spray design addresses that concern.
2. **Potential Market Size**
    - Roughly 40M Americans have severe allergies; 20M are under physician care.
    - About 6.5M epinephrine prescriptions are written, 3.2M are filled, each dose around $199.
    - This alone hints at a **$600M annual US market** if Neffy gains traction, not counting institutional buyers (schools, first responders, etc.).
3. **International Opportunities**
    - ARS is pursuing approvals/partners in major markets (China, Australia, Japan).
    - Worldwide severe allergy rates are growing, but pricing overseas is often lower.

---

### Patent & Technical Edge

- **Patent Until 2038**
    - They’re the only nasal epinephrine delivery system for a long while, which is huge if the product gains standard-of-care acceptance.
- **Nasal Spray Expertise**
    - ARS holds device patents + a permeation-enhancing drug (Intravail).
    - They’ve licensed the nasal sprayer tech for Narcan (opioid overdose drug) to Emergent BioSolutions (EBS).
